01. 本地数据存储与机密隔离（Local-First & Secrets Isolation） Local-First Storage & Secrets Isolation

HORD 遵循“本地优先（Local-First）”工程设计。在未登录云同步状态下，扩展完全离线运行：

HORD is built upon a strict "Local-First" architecture. When cloud sync is not enabled, the extension operates entirely on your local device:

  • 词库与学习数据： Vocabulary & Learning Data: 生词、释义、例句、用户笔记、艾宾浩斯复习进度、熟练度评分均保存在本机的 chrome.storage.local 与 IndexedDB 中。 Words, definitions, notes, spaced repetition progress, and mastery ratings are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB.
  • 自定义 AI 密钥（BYOK - Bring Your Own Key）： Custom AI API Keys (BYOK): 您在扩展中填写的个人 API Key（包括 OpenAI、Anthropic、DeepSeek、Gemini、Groq 等）仅保存在当前设备本地。HORD 数据层具备强制脱敏契约（Sanitization Contract），在任何云同步或导出流程中均自动剔除密钥字段，绝不上载至任何云端服务器。 Personal API Keys entered for custom AI providers remain strictly stored on your local device. Our sync layer employs an automated sanitization boundary that strips all provider secrets prior to sync, ensuring they are never uploaded.
  • 电子书正文： E-Book Full Text: 您导入阅读器的 TXT 或 EPUB 书籍正文仅缓存在本地 IndexedDB，绝不上传到服务器。 Full-text contents of imported TXT/EPUB e-books are cached only within local IndexedDB and never uploaded to remote servers.
  • 字幕翻译缓存： Subtitle Translation Cache: 视频字幕机器翻译缓存保存在本机存储（最多保留 30 条，写入时间先进先出 FIFO 淘汰，读取时做逻辑过期校验），不参与云同步。 Machine translation results for video subtitles are cached in local browser storage (maximum 30 items, FIFO eviction), and are never synchronized to the cloud.

02. 云端同步服务与网页访问信息披露（Cloud Sync & Web History Disclosure） Cloud Sync & Web History Transparency

云同步为主动登录功能。若您注册并登录了 HORD 账号，数据同步机制遵循以下严格规范：

Cloud sync is an optional, opt-in feature. When you register and log in to a HORD account, data synchronization operates under the following transparent rules:

⚠️ 关于来源网页 URL（sourceUrl）的关键说明： Important Note Regarding Source URLs (Web History):

当您在网页或视频网站上主动划词收藏单词或例句时，为保证您在其他设备（如 HORD Hub 或手机端）复习时能够追溯原汁原味的原生语境，该生词词条会附带记录发生划词行为的单条来源网址（sourceUrl）与页面标题（sourceTitle），并随生词元数据一并同步到您的云端个人词库。
【严正边界】：HORD 绝不监视、记录、搜集或上传您的任何未划词页面、全量浏览历史、搜索记录或会话 Cookie。没有发生主动划词行为的页面绝不会产生任何数据记录。

When you explicitly select and save a word or quote on a webpage or video, the extension captures only the single source URL (sourceUrl) and title (sourceTitle) of that specific page as metadata attached to the vocabulary card. This allows you to revisit the authentic sentence context across your devices (Hub/Mobile).
[Strict Boundary]: HORD NEVER monitors, logs, tracks, or uploads your comprehensive browsing history, non-word webpages, search queries, or cookies. Webpages without explicit user word collection trigger zero data logging.

传输与存储说明：同步数据通过行业标准的 HTTPS/TLS 加密通道传输，存储在由 HORD 托管的 Supabase 数据库专有数据表中。在此我们诚实说明：云端同步不是客户端端到端加密（Non-Client E2EE），数据经 gzip 压缩后由具备鉴权凭据的服务端安全存储，具有数据库运维权限的管理人员理论上可读取解压后数据，请勿将敏感涉密文本存入公开笔记。

Transmission & Storage: Data is synchronized over standard HTTPS/TLS and stored in dedicated tables within our hosted Supabase database. We maintain strict honesty: this sync is not client-side end-to-end encrypted (Non-E2EE); data is compressed with gzip and secured by server-side authentication controls.

03. 专业版授权与匿名设备校验（License Activation & Device Hash） License Verification & Anonymous Device Hash

为了校验 HORD Pro 激活码的设备绑定数量（最多支持 2 台设备），在激活授权时，客户端会通过浏览器环境属性计算生成一个单向不可逆的匿名哈希（Anonymous Device Hash）并提交给 License Worker。

To enforce the device activation limit for HORD Pro licenses (supporting up to 2 authorized devices), the client computes a one-way irreversible anonymous device hash based on general browser environment attributes during activation.

我们绝不会读取或采集您的真实硬件序列号、网卡 MAC 地址、IMEI、手机号码等敏感硬件特征。该匿名哈希仅用于设备数计算，无法反向解密出您的物理身份。

We never extract or inspect hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, IMEIs, or identifiable personal telemetry. The hash serves solely for license slot validation.

04. 第三方网络请求与外部交互（Third-Party Interactions） Third-Party Network Interactions

在您使用划词查词、发音与字幕功能时，会产生以下经过明确授权的网络交互：

When you actively look up words, play audio pronunciations, or display bilingual subtitles, the following minimal network interactions occur:

  • 词典与发音服务： Dictionary & Audio Services: 查询单词时，仅将所选英文词条发送至公开词典接口（如 有道、Google 翻译等）获取音标、释义与音频流。 When querying a word, only the selected vocabulary string is transmitted to public dictionary APIs (such as Youdao or Google) to retrieve phonetics and definitions.
  • 视频字幕伴侣（YouTube & Bilibili）： Video Subtitle Companions: 扩展仅向对应视频平台请求当前视频公开或当前登录用户授权可见的字幕轨道；翻译字幕时，仅按您指定的 Provider 顺序逐句发送字幕文本。 The companion only retrieves subtitle tracks publicly available or granted to your current session on the video host. Subtitle translation sends only caption strings to your chosen provider.

05. 您的权利：导出、备份与彻底删除（Data Portability & Erasure） Your Rights: Export & Permanent Deletion

您对自己的学习数据享有完整控制权：

You maintain complete ownership and control over your learning data:

  • 数据导出与便携性： Data Portability: 您可随时在扩展设置（Options）或 Hub 中将全部生词库与配置导出为标准的 JSON 或 CSV 文件。 You can export your complete vocabulary library and settings into JSON or CSV format at any time from the Settings dashboard.
  • 本地数据销毁： Local Erasure: 卸载 HORD 扩展或在浏览器中清除扩展存储，即可彻底销毁设备上的所有本地词库、缓存与配置。 Uninstalling the extension or clearing browser site data permanently deletes all locally stored words, caches, and configs.
  • 云端数据注销与清除： Cloud Data Deletion: 若需彻底清空或注销云端账号资产，您可随时发送邮件至支持邮箱，我们将在 3 个工作日内核实并从 Supabase 数据库中物理删除您的全部关联行。 To permanently delete your synchronized cloud data, send a request to our support email, and we will wipe your records from Supabase within 3 business days.
