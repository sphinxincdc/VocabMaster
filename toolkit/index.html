How does the width scaling avoid breaking responsive sidebars? ▼

Our custom layout engine continuously monitors geometric bounding boxes of side navigation bars and right-side widgets. It dynamically bounds target widths to `min(desired_width, available_viewport_space)`, gracefully falling back to avoid any content overlap.
